Horvath & Tremblay Continue National Expansion; Acquire Philly Brokerage

June 29, 2026
Philadelphia PA

Philadelphia PA — Less than a month after announcing further national expansion growth via the strategic integration with Gotham’s B6 Real Estate Advisors, Lynnfield-based Horvath & Tremblay announced it has deepened its footprint again, with the acquisition of Scope Commercial Real Estate Services (SCOPE), a leading commercial brokerage throughout the Philadelphia region, led by Phil Sharrow.

With the transaction, Sharrow, Founder and President, will strategize with H&T’s leadership on the firm’s Mid-Atlantic strategic growth as it advances deeper into the Northeast and beyond. The acquisition unites SCOPE’s deep-rooted Mid-Atlantic relationships with Horvath & Tremblay’s national platform, expanded service, and institutional-grade agent resources — creating a formidable presence for investment real estate in the Philadelphia market and beyond.

“H&T operates with the same urgency and discipline that we are accustomed to while having the resources and reach of the largest players in the industry. This is a great move for everybody involved,” extolled Sharrow. With this sale, H&T has expanded to 17 offices across the United States with just under 200 brokers. Its largest presence is in New England, followed by Chicago and the Midwest, while its Florida offices have also seen rapid growth.

The new H&T Philadelphia office will be located at 1650 Arch Street. Its new NYC office will open in the coming weeks, with approximately 6,000 square feet adjacent to Bryant Park. “Our growth is a direct reflection of what happens when you give talented brokers the right support, culture without bureaucracy, and a national network ... Phil and the SCOPE team are exactly the kind of operators we want leading these new markets,” said Executive Vice President and Horvath & Tremblay co-founder Todd Tremblay.

According to a release, Sharrow founded SCOPE in 2019 after leading Marcus & Millichap’s top-performing multifamily investment sales group in its Philadelphia office. Under Sharrow’s leadership, SCOPE built a reputation as a go-to regional brokerage for middle-market multifamily and development transactions — earning a seat at the table alongside institutional brokerage firms through a differentiated approach rooted in deep relationships and strong technical capabilities with local IQ.

“Phil and SCOPE have been highly respected in their market for some time. What they’ve done in a short period is exceptional. We look forward to building on that with the support of our NYC, NJ, and DC offices,” lauded Bob Horvath, Executive Vice President and co-founder of Horvath & Tremblay.
