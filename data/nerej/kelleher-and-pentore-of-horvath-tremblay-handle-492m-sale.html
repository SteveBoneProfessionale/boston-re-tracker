Kelleher and Pentore of Horvath & Tremblay handle $4.92 million sale of at 294-302 Windsor St. in Cambridge

Cambridge, MA Dennis Kelleher and John Pentore of Horvath & Tremblay have completed the sale of eight units consisting of seven residential apartments and one commercial space. Horvath & Tremblay exclusively represented the seller in the transaction. The asset sold for $4.92 million.

The mixed-use property is located at 294-302 Windsor St. in The Port neighborhood of the city. Situated in a high-density, urban-infill neighborhood, the walkable location is near Kendall Sq., Inman Sq., the Green Line at the Union Sq. Station, MIT, MGH, the Charles River Esplanade, and Downtown Boston.

294-302 Windsor St. consists of two buildings that contain a total of eight units, comprised of six three-bedroom/one-bathroom units, one two-bedroom/one-bathroom unit, and one first floor commercial unit with 7,765 s/f of living space in 11,343 s/f of total gross area on a 0.15-acre parcel. Additionally, there is a three-car garage located on the Hampshire St. side of the asset.

Building 1 (situated on the corner of Windsor and Hampshire St.) is a mixed-use building consisting of six units with five three-bedroom/one-bathroom units as well as Gypsy Café & Bar.

Building 2 (294 Windsor St.) is a two-unit property consisting of one three-bedroom/one-bathroom unit as well as one two-bedroom/one-bathroom unit.

294-302 Windsor St. features condo-quality upgrades and amenities throughout, offering an excellently maintained turnkey investment. Units feature newer kitchens with granite countertops, white cabinets, and stainless-steel appliances, including gas stoves and dishwashers, hardwood floors throughout, newer bathrooms as well as in-unit washers and dryers. Each of the units is separately metered for gas heat, hot water and electricity.

The property is located on Windsor St., near both Hampshire St. and Broadway in the high-density, urban-infill neighborhood of The Port. The property is 0.4 miles from Kendall Sq., 0.4 miles from Inman Sq., and 0.9 miles from Union Sq. The location is also close to the employment centers of Kendall Sq., Downtown Boston, multiple public transportation options, and numerous shopping, dining, entertainment, and outdoor recreation options.
